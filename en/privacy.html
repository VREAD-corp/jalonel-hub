What this home page collects

No personal data: no form, no account, no cookie, no third-party tracker. GitHub, the host, may log technical connection data (including IP addresses) for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Audience and behaviour measurement

We count visits to jalonel.com and how its pages are used, anonymously and in aggregate: page views, clicks to the sites, referring domain and utm parameters, scroll depth, sections reached, time to the first button click, outbound links (by domain), technical errors (as an anonymous fingerprint), page speed (Core Web Vitals) and repeated clicks. Only daily totals are kept, in a READ FRANCE SAS spreadsheet hosted by Google. No cookie, no identifier, no IP address, no personal data, no cross-site tracking. Crawlers and automated browsers are counted apart or ignored.

A/B tests. The home page headline may change from one day to the next. The version depends on the date only, with no storage, and is never linked to a person.

Opting out. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is measured.

Each Jalonel site has its own privacy page describing what it processes (for example the data of an order).
